"Know Your Network" — Lesson 2: Reading a Subnet Mask

HOST: Okay — if I hand you an address and a mask, what's the first thing
you look at?

HOST: The mask tells you which part of the address is the network, and
which part is the host. That's it. That's the whole trick.

Segment 1: Splitting the Address

HOST: Take 255.255.255.0. Three whole octets are network, one is host.
That's a /24.

HOST: A /25 just moves the line halfway through the last octet instead of
stopping at the boundary.
